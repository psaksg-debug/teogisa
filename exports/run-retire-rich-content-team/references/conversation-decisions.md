## Brand and reader

- Company and project: 애드블스 운영 `퇴.기.사`
- Brand promise: turn retirement anxiety into numbers, official evidence, and one action the reader can take today.
- Primary reader: Korean workers and retirees roughly one year before retirement through two years afterward, usually ages 45–64.
- Core situations: retirement preparation, immediate post-retirement administration and cash flow, re-employment, small income experiments, health, and local support.
- Avoid: get-rich-quick framing, guaranteed income, stock picks, medical diagnosis, and unsupported tax or legal conclusions.

The five content pillars are:

1. Retirement money and living-cost defense
2. Official programs and rights
3. New work and income rebuilding
4. Healthy transition to a 100-year life
5. Local information, practical tools, and workbooks

Treat AI, video, checklists, calculators, and ebooks as formats, not reader-problem categories.

## Workflow and gates

Standard flow:

`idea → brief_ready → assigned → draft → editorial_review → quality_review → policy_review → scheduled → published → retrospected`

Return correctable work to `needs_revision`. Put technical failures in `failed`. Put deception, rights, policy, or unresolved commercial issues in `blocked`.

Required evidence gates:

- G0 brief: reader, question, action, difference, source candidates, risk, owner, deadline.
- G1 editorial: sufficient explanation, headings, conditions, date basis, official sources, checked numbers, next action, original example or table.
- G2 originality and safety: duplication check, no guarantee or diagnosis language, manual review if automated checks are unavailable.
- G3 quality design: brand, hierarchy, mobile, keyboard and screen reader, image caption and alt, tags, author, links.
- G4 policy and schedule: approver and timestamp, checklist version, timezone, slug and link validation, rollback state.
- G5 post-publication: real URL 200, metadata, OG image, links, mobile, zero critical defects.

Queue rules: never silently delete or auto-publish failed work; preserve sources, input, error, attempts, owner, due time, and next action. Retry only transient technical errors. Escalate critical safety issues immediately.

## Operating boundaries

- AI creates drafts; humans or accountable roles approve publication.
- Prepare UTM links and channel copy without external posting.
- External account posting, paid ads, messages, remote database changes, deployment, DNS, and search-console mutations require appropriate explicit authority.
- Preserve user changes in a dirty worktree and avoid unrelated rewrites.
- Build success is not public verification. Deployment success is not user acceptance.
- For public changes, verify the actual URL and report local, remote, live, and accepted states separately.
