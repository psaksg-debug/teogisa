# Facebook ad-operations side-income and ebook campaign

## Campaign position

Define the opportunity as supporting Meta advertising operations for a small business or solo operator. Explicitly distinguish it from clicking ads for pay, account rental, fake engagement, affiliate spam, or effortless income.

Recommended article promise: explain the work, required skills, customer and operator costs, account and data responsibility, and a seven- to fourteen-day validation before spending meaningful money or taking a client.

`페이스북 광고 부업 현실: 광고비·필요 역량·첫 고객 전 확인할 7가지`

Recommended slug:

`facebook-ads-side-hustle-reality`

## Required structure

1. Paid clicking versus legitimate ad-operation support
2. Actual workflow: goal, creative, landing page, launch, measurement, report
3. Who pays and what the smallest service includes
4. Skills, learning time, ad spend, tools, fee, refunds, and unpaid work
5. A seven-day sample exercise before the first client
6. Five real prospects and a fourteen-day demand test
7. Revise and stop conditions
8. Account access, privacy, policy, intellectual-property, tax, and record risks
9. Fit checklist and relevant free internal resources
10. Optional ebook contents, exclusions, price, sample, delivery, refund, and contact

Include at least one responsibility table, one net-income worksheet, one fourteen-day calendar, and one pre-client checklist.

## Ebook relationship

The free article must be useful by itself. The ebook may save execution time with:

- prospect interview questions;
- scope and responsibility table;
- account and permission checklist;
- budget and response log;
- weekly client report;
- first outreach examples;
- continue, revise, and stop worksheet.

Before the first product CTA, state plainly that 퇴.기.사 produces or sells the ebook. Do not disguise owned-product promotion as an independent recommendation.

Require the actual product title, seller, URL, price, contents, sample, format, delivery, update scope, refund terms, contact route, and any affiliate compensation. If missing, leave the article in `needs_revision`; do not schedule it.

## Prohibited framing

Do not use or imply:

- anyone can do it;
- ten minutes a day;
- guaranteed monthly income;
- automatic income;
- guaranteed ad approval;
- no account-suspension risk;
- no ad budget required;
- AI does all the work;
- immediate clients;
- an official Meta relationship without proof.

Do not instruct readers to share passwords, rent accounts, evade review, fabricate testimonials, copy creative, or conceal data collection.

Meta may review ad text, creative, targeting, and destination pages. Treat the article and product landing page as one compliance surface if the article later becomes a paid-ad destination. Do not guarantee review time or approval.

## Suggested handoff

- Planning: angle, reader, claims, CTA, risks
- Editorial: evidence pack, article, example calculations
- Quality design: original image, tables, mobile CTA
- Management: relationship disclosure, product terms, link and purchase QA, schedule
- Promotion: organic copy, UTM, indexing, 7-day and 28-day review

Keep public article publication separate from an external Facebook post and from paid campaign activation. Each external action needs its own verified state and authority.
